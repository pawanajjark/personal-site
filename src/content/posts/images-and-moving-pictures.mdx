Some things are easier to show. An image can sit inside a piece of writing without turning the whole page into a gallery. A caption adds the bit of context that the picture cannot carry by itself.

## An image with room to breathe

The image below is an original geometric color study made for this development sample. It is not a photograph or a personal memory. On a larger screen, it extends slightly beyond the reading column. On a phone, it fits the page.

Local images are processed into responsive sizes during the build, so a smaller screen can request a smaller file. The text stays at a comfortable reading width even when the image has more space.

## Let the words continue

Media should fit into the flow of a piece. A reader can look, watch, and then continue without losing their place. Keep descriptions useful, captions brief, and give speech-led videos captions or a transcript.
