I want to start documenting more of my life, work, and the things I learn.

Sometimes that will mean writing a full article. Sometimes it will be a short note about something I figured out, an idea I’m still working through, or a photo or video I want to keep with the story.

I started by looking for a simple way to put all of that online. There are plenty of blogging tools and personal-site templates, but I kept coming back to a particular kind of site: a short introduction, readable text, and links to things someone has written.

[Dead Simple Sites](https://deadsimplesites.com/) helped me narrow down what I liked. [Steph Ango’s site](https://stephango.com/) was especially close to the feeling I wanted. It has personality, but the writing still has room to breathe.

I want something similar in spirit. Simple, a little modern, and easy to add to.

This also gives me permission to write at different lengths. A small discovery does not need to become a long article to deserve a place here. If I learn something useful, I can write it down while it is still fresh. If a subject needs more time, I can return to it later.

Work will be part of this site, but so will life outside it. I want to leave room for both, along with the ideas and questions that connect them.

For now, the first step is to make a place for it all. This is that place.
