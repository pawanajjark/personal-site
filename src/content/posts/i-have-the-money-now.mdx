I have 53 Hot Wheels now. I recorded myself scrolling through the collection recently, and seeing them all together made me think about how few toys I had growing up.

We couldn't buy toys often. I used to play with random things I could find, or with toys that belonged to other kids. You find ways to play with whatever is around.

Playing with other kids meant making friends and getting comfortable around people. I think that helped me become social. I also feel like some of my people pleasing might have started there. I cared about being included. Looking back, I wonder how often I went along with things because I wanted people to keep liking me.

I don't have a clear answer to that. It's just something I've been thinking about.

Growing up, I was told to study, get good marks, get into a better college, and then get a good job. I could enjoy life later. I heard that enough that it became the order in which I thought things were supposed to happen.

Now I have an SDE job. It pays well. I've reached the part where I was supposed to get to enjoy myself, and I'm spending some of that money on Hot Wheels.

I like being able to buy a car because I like it. As a kid, wanting a toy and being able to get it were very different things. Having my own money has changed that for me.

I've also always loved taking pictures. Whenever I see someone with a camera, I ask if I can take a picture with it. Then come all my questions about the buttons and how things work. I want to try it myself. I'm happy to ask random questions until I understand a little more.

Recently, I bought a Sony Cybershot DSC-T20. It's an old digicam from 2007. I love taking pictures with it.

I like capturing moments, and now I have this little camera of my own to do it with. The excitement I have when someone lets me try their camera is still there with mine. I get to keep using it and figuring it out.

I think this is what looking after my inner child looks like for me right now. I'm buying some of the things I wanted and giving myself time to enjoy them. I'm still curious about cameras, and apparently I can happily own 53 little cars.

I wanted a place on this site for that part of my life too. I spend a lot of time working and learning things for my job. Outside of that, I'm taking pictures with an old Sony and adding toy cars to my collection. Those are things I want to remember when I come back and read this.

I have the money now. I'm enjoying being able to do this for myself.
