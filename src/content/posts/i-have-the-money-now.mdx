My cousins came over from my village recently, and I took them to Mall of Asia. We were walking around, exploring each floor, when I spotted Hamleys and went in.

Then I saw they had Hot Wheels. I thought, “Wow, let me look at some.”

While I was browsing, I noticed a kid next to me looking through the cars too. He picked one, and I helped him pick two more. I think I saw myself in him.

I didn't have many toys growing up. We couldn't buy them often. I used to play with random things I could find, or with toys that belonged to other kids. You find ways to play with whatever is around.

I think playing with other kids helped me become social. I also wonder if some of my people-pleasing started there. I cared about being included. Looking back, I wonder how often I went along with things because I wanted people to keep liking me.

I don't have a clear answer to that. It's just something I've been thinking about.

As a kid, wanting a toy and being able to get it were very different things.

Growing up, I was told to study, get good marks, get into a better college, and then get a good job. I could enjoy life later. That became the order in which I thought things were supposed to happen.

Now I have an SDE job. It pays well. I guess this is the “later” I kept hearing about.

I bought three Hot Wheels for myself that day. I have 53 now. What felt good was being able to buy them with my own money, just because I liked them.

I've also always loved taking pictures. Whenever I see someone with a camera, I ask if I can take a picture with it. Then come all my questions about the buttons and how things work. I want to try it myself. I'm happy to ask random questions until I understand a little more.

Recently, I bought a Sony Cybershot DSC-T20. It's an old digicam from 2007. I love taking pictures with it.

I like capturing moments, and now I have this little camera of my own to do it with. The excitement I have when someone lets me try their camera is still there with mine. I get to keep using it and figuring it out.

I think this is what looking after my inner child looks like for me right now. I’m buying some of the things I wanted and giving myself time to enjoy them. I’m still curious about cameras, and apparently I can happily own 53 little cars.

I wanted a place on this site for that part of my life too. I spend a lot of time working and learning things for my job. Outside of that, I’m taking pictures with an old Sony and adding toy cars to my collection. Those are things I want to remember when I come back and read this.
