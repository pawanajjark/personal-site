# Development media

`src/assets/light-study.png`, `light-study-poster.jpg`, and `light-study.mp4` are original geometric demonstration assets created for this project on 1 November 2026. They show shapes rather than a personal photograph or experience. No external photos or video were reused. They can be used, edited, or removed with the site.

Regenerate them with `sh scripts/create-demo-media.sh` (requires FFmpeg with libx264). The video is six seconds, silent, H.264 MP4, 1280 × 720, 24 fps, with fast-start metadata. It is intentionally tiny so the example can remain in Git; put large real recordings on external storage.

DM Sans is locally bundled through `@fontsource-variable/dm-sans`, under the SIL Open Font License. The font copyright and license are preserved at `/licenses/DM-Sans-OFL.txt`. No Astro Nano code was reused.


The three SVG files in `src/assets/studies/` are original flat geometric demonstration artworks made for the image-fan preview. Circles, nested arches, and rectangular color blocks use the site’s warm palette. They are not personal photos. They may be used, edited, or removed with the site. No ReactDrops demo assets or component source were copied; the fan arrangement is visual inspiration from https://reactdrops.dev/components/gallery/fan-gallery.
